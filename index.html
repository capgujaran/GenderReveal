Let’s play, baby.

  1. Build your answer. Drag letters from the scrambled row into the boxes below. Or tap a letter, then tap an answer box.
  2. Rearrange freely. Drag between answer boxes to swap letters. Drag a letter back up to remove it, or use Clear to start again.
  3. Check your guess. Tap Submit. A correct answer shows the word and picture; an incorrect answer lets you try again.
  4. Need a little help? Hint shows a picture. Reveal answer shows the word. Use Next word to continue through all 20.

Play at your own pace. Each letter can be used once, including repeated letters.
Use H for Hint, R for Reveal, and the arrow keys to move between words. Escape cancels a selected letter. Press F for full screen.The timer starts on your first action and pauses when you switch games. You can save any score at the end. Only correct submissions add to your score; revealed answers do not.
